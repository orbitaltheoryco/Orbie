Don't know what Nightscout is or how to set it up?

Nightscout is a free, open-source project that takes the readings from your CGM (Dexcom, Libre, etc.) and puts them on a private web page of your own — that's the URL this form is asking for. It's built and maintained by the diabetes community, not by Orbie.

You'll need your own Nightscout site already running before you can use it here — this page can't create one for you. Setup usually takes 15–30 minutes and free hosting options exist.

If your Nightscout requires a token, use a read-only one — Orbie only ever reads, never writes. Your link expires automatically after 30 days of no visits.
Which login do I use? (LibreLinkUp)

Orbie reads FreeStyle Libre glucose through LibreLinkUp, Abbott's free app that lets family and friends follow a Libre user. You need a LibreLinkUp login — it is not the login you use in the LibreLink app or on LibreView.

Already follow someone in LibreLinkUp (or yourself)? Use that email and password.

Not set up yet?

  1. The person wearing the sensor opens the LibreLink app, goes to the menu → Connected Apps → LibreLinkUp → Add Connection, and enters the email address to invite. (Menu names can vary a little between app versions.)
  2. On your phone, install LibreLinkUp, sign up with that email, and accept the invitation.
  3. Open LibreLinkUp once and check you can see the readings. If it asks you to accept updated terms, accept them there first — Orbie can't do that for you.

Tip: you can invite a second email address of your own just for Orbie, so you never have to hand over your main login.

What happens to this login

Orbie keeps your LibreLinkUp email and password encrypted on its server so it can check for new readings for you. It's used only to fetch your glucose, and you can delete it at any time from your Orbie page.

Encryption protects against a leak of the database, but the server does hold the key to sign in as you — so please use a separate follower email rather than your main login.

This uses an unofficial connection to LibreLinkUp. Orbie isn't affiliated with Abbott, and it may stop working if Abbott changes things. The weekly recap isn't available with LibreLinkUp.
